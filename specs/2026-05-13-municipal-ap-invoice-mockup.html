Sum-Match Validation
Decides whether the sum of the coded-line amounts must reconcile to a header field before the invoice can advance, and what happens on mismatch.
GL Segment Format
Coding restriction rules use a segment-based pattern. Define how GL account strings are split — most municipal charts of accounts use a 4-segment hyphenated pattern like 1111-2222-3333-4444.
Department Coding Rules
Filters which GL accounts a coder may select based on which department they belong to. Multiple rules per department combine with OR. Coders without a matching rule cannot select that GL on a coded line; the dropdown hides non-matching values, and validation enforces the rule on submit. Roles with the Bypass coding restrictions permission (Admins, AP Clerks) ignore these rules entirely.
